Map Builder

Make an offline map pack for the Meshtastic CYD map screen, straight from your browser. Pick an area, build, and copy the file to the node's SD card. No install, no account.

1. Pick the area

Click the map to move the centre. The green box is what goes in the pack.

2. Build

Ready.

3. Put it on the node

  1. Make a folder called maps in the root of the node's microSD card.
  2. Copy the .map file into it. Add as many as you like: neighbouring areas join up.
  3. Put the card in the node and turn Bluetooth off (the card is only mounted then). Open the map screen.

Needs firmware 2.8.1.9d8d166 or newer. For a whole state or region, use tools/make_map_region.py on a PC instead. See the README.
