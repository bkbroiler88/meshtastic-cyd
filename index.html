Meshtastic CYD — Web Flasher

Flash the LCDWIKI CYD + E22 builds, a Heltec V3 spectrum build and the Piglet XIAO node straight from your browser. Use Chrome or Edge on desktop — Web Serial isn't available in Firefox or Safari.

ST7789 panel

The common LCDWIKI 2.8" with the ST7789 controller. This is the build tested on hardware.

ILI9341 panel

Same board with the ILI9341 controller. Interchangeable modules ship under one name; pick this if ST7789 shows a blank or scrambled screen.

Built but not yet verified on hardware — if it misbehaves, try the ST7789 build.

LCDWIKI 3.5" (ST7796)

The 480×320 LCDWIKI 3.5" ESP32-32E with an E22 on the same wiring scheme. Tested on hardware.

Heltec V3 + spectrum analyzer

Stock Heltec WiFi LoRa 32 V3 with the on-device spectrum analyzer (LoRa Actions → Spectrum) and Radio Tuning added. Its bare SX1262 reads close to true dBm, so it makes a good measuring receiver.

Piglet (XIAO ESP32-S3 DIY)

A Piglet wardriver repurposed as a Meshtastic node: XIAO ESP32-S3 + Wio-SX1262, 0.96" OLED, receive-only GPS. Comes with Piglet, a pet pig on his own screen who gets hungry, cheers up when the mesh is busy, gets fitter when you walk him and plays fetch with nearby nodes. Long-press on his screen for his menu. Needs the wiring in the README.

Installing erases the device. Wi-Fi credentials, node name and channels are wiped; reconfigure from the Meshtastic phone app over Bluetooth after flashing. Maps live on the SD card and are untouched.
